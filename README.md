One funny line, under 200 characters, pitching a terrible pizza topping, written by a program that prints it to /output/entry.txt:

> Try our new pizza topped with one raw egg — it hatches in the oven, the chick looks up at you, and suddenly you're the topping.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## How it was picked

12 candidates, tournament self-judged head-to-head on the brief's criteria (pitches a terrible topping, funny with a surprising turn, single line, vivid and specific, sounds like a real pitch). The round-1 champion (a whole raw potato staring back like it didn't order this either) lost head-to-head against the strongest visible lines in the field — pickled herring, gas-station sushi, durian — so a fresh round was run. The round-2 champion is the raw egg: three causal beats ending in a role reversal, and the "hatch" concept appears in none of the 1,866 visible entries.
